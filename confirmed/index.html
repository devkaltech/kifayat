If it says the link expired

Confirmation links are single-use and time-limited, so an old one — or one you have already opened — will report an error even though nothing is wrong with your account.

Try signing in anyway. If your address was already confirmed, it will simply work. If it genuinely did not go through, sign up again from the app and use the newest email; the older links stop working once a new one is sent.
