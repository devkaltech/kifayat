Kifayat

Your money, on your phone, and nowhere else.

Kifayat is a private budget planner and expense tracker. No bank logins, no ads, no tracking — your ledger is encrypted on your device.

What it does

  • Track income and expenses in seconds
  • Budget by category, and see where the money actually goes
  • Multiple accounts — cash, bank, card, savings — in one balance
  • Set salary, rent and subscriptions once, and let them post on schedule
  • Export an elegant PDF statement or a CSV, any time
  • Lock the app with your fingerprint
  • English and Arabic

Private by design

Kifayat is local-first. Your transactions live in an encrypted database on your phone and work with no connection at all. When you sign in, they sync over HTTPS to your own private account — and nothing else.

Kifayat has no advertising, no usage analytics and no tracking of any kind. Your data is never sold and never shared for marketing. You can export everything, or delete everything, whenever you want.

The app does send us a report when it crashes, so faults get found and fixed — with your amounts, notes and email address stripped out before it is sent. The privacy policy sets out exactly what such a report can and cannot contain.

Your data, your call
